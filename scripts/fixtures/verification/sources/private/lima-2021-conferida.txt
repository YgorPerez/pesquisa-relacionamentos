Results

Of the 1,204 respondents who reported a current partner, 18.5 percent
met their partner through a dating app. Among those aged 18 to 24 the
share rose to 24%, while relation-
ship satisfaction did not differ between groups (r = .04).
Satisfaction was the mean of three items rated from 1 to 7.
